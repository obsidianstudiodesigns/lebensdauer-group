Sectors

Where our work lands

Three core building sectors, backed by our own civils, renovation and project management capability.

The full capability.

Service 01

Commercial construction

Offices, retail, hospitality and mixed-use developments — creating functional spaces that inspire success.

We build commercial property that works for the business inside it and for the person who has to let it. That means getting the structure, services coordination and floor-plate efficiency right early, and delivering a finish that still looks new after its first few tenants.

  • New-build shells and full turnkey fit-out
  • Structural steel and reinforced concrete frames
  • Building services coordination
  • Façades, glazing and cladding
  • Tenant installations and refits
  • External works and parking
Service 02

Industrial construction

Warehousing, factories and distribution facilities — building stronger foundations for industry and growth.

Industrial work is unforgiving: floor tolerances, clear spans, hardstanding and vehicle movements all have to be right the first time. We build around how the facility will actually be operated, and we leave room for the expansion you'll want in five years.

  • Portal-frame and steel-framed warehousing
  • Power-floated and laser-screed industrial slabs
  • Hardstanding, yards and heavy-vehicle access
  • Factory and production facility builds
  • Office and ablution blocks within facilities
  • Plant plinths and specialist bases
Service 03

High-end residential construction

Bespoke homes. Exceptional quality. Timeless elegance.

A private home is the most scrutinised thing we build, and rightly so — you live in every millimetre of it. We work closely with architects and interior designers, protect the detail through the programme, and keep the site clean, quiet and predictable.

  • New bespoke homes from architects' drawings
  • Joinery, stone and specialist finishes
  • Pools, decks and landscaped terraces
  • Home automation and services first-fix
  • Guest cottages and outbuildings
  • Estate and security-complex compliance
Service 04

Renovations & alterations

Revitalising existing spaces with precision and expertise.

Working inside an existing building is a different craft: unknown conditions, live operations and neighbours who did not ask for a building site. We survey properly before we price, phase the work around your occupation, and make good like we were never there.

  • Structural alterations and openings
  • Extensions and additional storeys
  • Full refurbishment and re-fit
  • Phased works on occupied premises
  • Heritage-sensitive repairs
  • Waterproofing and remedial works
Service 05

Civil infrastructure works

Building the foundations for tomorrow — the groundwork everything above it depends on.

Our civils capability means the platform, services and access are handled by the same contractor who puts the building on top of them. Fewer interfaces, fewer arguments about levels, and one programme instead of two.

  • Bulk earthworks and site platforms
  • Roads, kerbing and surfacing
  • Stormwater and sewer reticulation
  • Water, bulk services and connections
  • Retaining structures and terracing
  • Site clearance and demolition
Service 06

Project management

End-to-end management. Seamless delivery.

Whether we hold the building contract or not, we can run the project: appointing and coordinating consultants, driving the programme, controlling cost and reporting it all back to you in language that doesn't need a quantity surveyor to decode.

  • Feasibility studies and budget setting
  • Consultant appointment and coordination
  • Programme development and control
  • Cost management and variation control
  • Subcontractor procurement
  • Handover, close-out and O&M packs

How we work

Concept to completion

The same four stages on a R2m renovation and a R200m development. Only the detail scales.

  1. Consultation & feasibility

    We walk the site, understand the brief and the budget, and tell you honestly what is achievable before anyone commits.

  2. Design, costing & programme

    Drawings, bills of quantities and a realistic construction programme — coordinated with your architects and engineers.

  3. Construction

    A dedicated site team, disciplined subcontractor management and quality checks at every stage, with regular progress reporting.

  4. Handover & aftercare

    Snag lists closed out, certificates and manuals in hand, and a team that still answers the phone once the site fence comes down.

Quality. Integrity. Reliability.
