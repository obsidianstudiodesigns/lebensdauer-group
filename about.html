About the group

Building tomorrow,
today.

Lebensdauer — German for lifespan. It is the word we build to: structures that outlast the people who commissioned them.

Our story

20 years of excellence in construction

Established in 2017, Lebensdauer Group was founded on two decades of combined site experience gathered long before the company carried a name.

That background shapes how we work now. We take on commercial developments, industrial facilities, high-end homes, renovations and civil works — and we run each of them with the same discipline: understand the brief properly, price it honestly, programme it realistically, then build it exactly as agreed.

We don't just build structures. We build lasting partnerships and a better future for the communities our projects sit in. Most of our work now comes from clients we have already built for, or the people they recommended us to.

What drives us

Three things
we don't bend on

Every decision on site gets measured against these. If it fails one of them, we do it again.

01

Craftsmanship

The finish is the part the client lives with for the next thirty years. We hold our trades to a standard, check the work before it is covered up, and fix what isn't right while it is still cheap to fix.

02

Innovation

Better methods, better sequencing, better materials. We look for the buildable solution rather than the traditional one, and we bring it to the table early — while it can still save you money.

03

Partnership

Consultants, subcontractors and clients on the same side of the table. Clear reporting, honest variation discussions and no adversarial games when something unexpected comes out of the ground.
